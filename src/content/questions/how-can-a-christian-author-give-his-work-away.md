Dedicate the work to the public domain, say so plainly in the work itself, publish a free digital copy, and let anyone print or translate it. Then seek support as a ministry rather than income as a seller.

## Proofs
- Matthew 10:8
- Proverbs 23:23
- Matthew 5:15-16
- 2 Corinthians 9:7

Practical steps:

1. **Dedicate it.** Add a public domain dedication such as Creative Commons Zero (CC0). A simple statement in the front of the book or the footer of the site is enough, for example: "To the extent possible under law, the author has waived all copyright and related rights to this work."
2. **Say it plainly.** Tell readers in ordinary words that they may copy, print, translate and share without asking.
3. **Publish a free digital copy** where anyone can find it, on your site or on a site that collects freely given books.
4. **Let printing be printing.** If people want paper copies, use print-on-demand at cost, or let others print and sell at cost.
5. **Avoid traditional publishing contracts** that require you to transfer copyright to a publisher. Once you sign, you cannot give the work away.
6. **Seek support honestly.** Ask your church and partners to support the writing as ministry, without promising perks or bonus content in return.
7. **Leave existing restrictions behind.** If you have published under copyright before, ask your publisher whether the rights can revert to you, and then release them.

This website follows the same approach: everything on it is in the public domain.
