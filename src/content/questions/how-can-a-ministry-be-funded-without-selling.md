By asking God's people for support, giving the work away, being honest about needs, and trusting the Lord to provide. Churches, partners and the minister's own labor can all supply what fees would have.

## Proofs
- Philippians 4:19
- Matthew 6:33
- 2 Corinthians 9:7-8
- 3 John 8
- Acts 20:34-35

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Many ministers say, "I agree with the principle, but how would we pay for anything?" The New Testament gives several answers, and they can be combined:

1. **The local church.** Ask your elders and congregation to fund the work as a ministry of the church. A church that will not support a ministry may not yet be convinced of its value, and that is worth knowing.
2. **Partners.** Invite believers and churches to give regularly. Tell them plainly what the work is and what it needs (Philippians 4:15-16).
3. **Your own labor.** Like Paul, work another job and serve freely in the remaining time (Acts 20:34-35).
4. **Prefunding.** Raise support before a project begins, so that the finished work can be released freely.
5. **Lower costs.** Free ministry often costs less: no sales platforms, payment processing, marketing or licensing.

None of this guarantees comfort. It does put the minister where Jesus put the twelve, dependent on the Lord of the harvest. "Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you" (Matthew 6:33). "And my God will supply all your needs according to His glorious riches in Christ Jesus" (Philippians 4:19).
