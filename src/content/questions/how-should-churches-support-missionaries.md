Churches should send and supply missionaries generously, as partners in their work, so that they can preach without taking anything from those they are trying to reach.

## Proofs
- 3 John 5-8
- Philippians 4:15-16
- Romans 15:24
- Acts 13:2-3
- 1 Corinthians 9:14

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

John's words to Gaius describe the pattern: the missionaries "went out on behalf of the Name, accepting nothing from the Gentiles. Therefore we ought to support such men, so that we may be fellow workers for the truth" (3 John 7-8).

Notice the logic. Because the missionaries take nothing from the people they evangelize, the churches must supply them. Free ministry abroad depends on generous giving at home.

The Philippians did this for Paul "again and again" (Philippians 4:16). The church at Antioch sent Paul and Barnabas out with prayer and the laying on of hands (Acts 13:3). Churches that send missionaries should therefore:

- give regularly and sacrificially, not leaving missionaries to fund themselves through sales or fees in their field;
- pray for them and stay in contact, as partners rather than distant donors;
- encourage them to give their teaching, translations and materials away freely in the places they serve.

Supporting a missionary is not charity toward a needy individual. It is sharing in the work of Christ.
