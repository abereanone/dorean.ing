Parachurch ministries should serve under and alongside local churches, funded by churches and believers who give as partners, so that their teaching and help can be given freely.

## Proofs
- 3 John 5-8
- Philippians 4:15-16
- Acts 13:2-3
- 1 Corinthians 16:1-3

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Many good ministries operate outside a single local church: mission agencies, publishers, counseling centers, schools, campus ministries. Because they lack a congregation that gives every week, they are often tempted to fund themselves by selling: subscriptions, tickets, products, fees for service.

The New Testament pattern is different. Paul's mission was sent by a church (Acts 13:2-3), supported by partner churches (Philippians 4:15-16), and carried relief from churches to churches (1 Corinthians 16:1-3). Traveling workers were supplied by believers so that they could take nothing from those they served (3 John 7-8).

Parachurch ministries can follow that pattern by:

- **working under the oversight of a local church** where possible, so that the ministry is part of a church's mission;
- **seeking partner churches** that build the ministry into their budgets;
- **inviting individual partners** to give, honestly and without manipulation;
- **raising support before launching a work**, rather than recovering costs by sales afterward;
- **giving their materials away**, so that donors fund the work and recipients receive it freely.
