No. Rejoice wherever Christ is preached, and benefit from good teaching even when it comes with a price. At the same time, gently and lovingly encourage those ministries to give their work freely.

## Proofs
- Philippians 1:15-18
- 2 Timothy 2:24-25
- Matthew 18:15
- Matthew 17:27

Paul wrote from prison about preachers whose motives were poor. Some preached "out of envy and rivalry... not sincerely" (Philippians 1:15-17). His response: "What then is the issue? Just this: that in every way, whether by false motives or true, Christ is preached. And in this I rejoice" (Philippians 1:18).

That should shape how we treat ministries that charge. Many of them teach the truth faithfully and have helped us. Refusing to read a good book or attend a sound conference because it costs money is not required by the dorean principle, which binds the giver, not the receiver.

But love also speaks up. Faithful steps include:

- **Talk to the minister privately** and respectfully, as a brother (Matthew 18:15).
- **Explain the principle from Scripture**, with patience: "A servant of the Lord must not be quarrelsome, but must be kind to everyone, able to teach, and forbearing" (2 Timothy 2:24).
- **Offer help**: suggest support models, or offer to give toward the cost of releasing the work freely.
- **Never pirate** what has been withheld, so that the appeal is not undercut by bad example.
