He sent them to preach and heal without charge and without money of their own, to stay with those who welcomed them, and to trust that the Lord of the harvest would provide for His workers.

## Proofs
- Matthew 10:5-11
- Matthew 9:37-38
- Luke 10:1-9

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

The setting matters. Just before the mission, Jesus told His disciples, "Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest" (Matthew 9:38). The workers belong to the Lord of the harvest, and He is the one who provides for them.

Then come the instructions:

1. **Preach and heal** (Matthew 10:7-8).
2. **Do not charge.** "Freely you have received; freely give" (Matthew 10:8).
3. **Do not fund yourselves in advance.** No gold, silver or copper, no bag or spare tunic (Matthew 10:9-10).
4. **Expect provision.** "The worker is worthy of his provisions" (Matthew 10:10).
5. **Stay with a worthy host.** "Find out who is worthy there and stay at his house until you move on" (Matthew 10:11).

The provision came through people who welcomed the disciples because they welcomed their message and their Lord, not as a fee from the sick who were healed or the crowds who heard. Jesus did not set the disciples' needs against free ministry. He made free ministry the rule and promised the provision alongside it.
