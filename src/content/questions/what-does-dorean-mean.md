Dorean means "as a gift," "without payment," or "freely." It describes something given without cost to the one who receives it.

## Proofs
- Matthew 10:8
- Romans 3:24
- 2 Corinthians 11:7
- Revelation 22:17

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

The word appears in several places that show its meaning plainly:

- **Our salvation.** We "are justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus" (Romans 3:24). Justification is not purchased; it is a gift.
- **Paul's preaching.** "I preached the gospel of God to you free of charge" (2 Corinthians 11:7). Same word.
- **The last invitation of the Bible.** "let the one who is thirsty come, and the one who desires the water of life drink freely" (Revelation 22:17).
- **Jesus' command.** "Freely you have received; freely give" (Matthew 10:8).

The same word describes how God gives salvation and how His servants are to give the message of salvation. That is not an accident. The way the gospel is delivered should match what the gospel is.

The word can also mean "without a cause." Jesus said His enemies "hated Me without reason" (John 15:25), using the same word. Something done *dorean* is not earned and not paid for.
