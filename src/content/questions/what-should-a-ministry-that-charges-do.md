Turn from the practice gladly, with a plan: announce that the ministry will be free, ask the church and partners for support, separate real material costs from ministry, release written work freely, and trust God through the transition.

## Proofs
- Proverbs 28:13
- Luke 19:8
- Matthew 6:33
- Philippians 4:19
- 2 Corinthians 7:10

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Changing a funding model can be costly, and fear of that cost keeps many ministers where they are. But "whoever confesses and renounces them will find mercy" (Proverbs 28:13). When Zacchaeus met Jesus, he did not ask for a gradual plan; he stood up and changed (Luke 19:8). Still, wisdom plans well.

A practical path:

1. **Settle it before God.** Study the Scriptures on the subject, and talk with your elders.
2. **Announce the change.** Tell those you serve that ministry will now be offered freely, and when.
3. **Ask for support.** Bring the need to your church and to partners. Many churches will fund what they had not realized needed funding.
4. **Separate costs from ministry.** Keep charging at cost, if necessary, for things that are not ministry: meals, lodging, paper.
5. **Release your work.** Dedicate past and future teaching, books and songs to the public domain where you hold the rights.
6. **Allow a transition period** if commitments or contracts require it, but do not let it become permanent.
7. **Trust the Lord.** "Seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added unto you" (Matthew 6:33).
