Because it was freely given to us. The gospel is God's gift, and the Spirit gives understanding of it freely, so those who minister it are stewards of a gift, not sellers of a product.

## Proofs
- Matthew 10:8
- 1 Corinthians 2:12
- 1 Corinthians 4:1-2
- Isaiah 55:1
- Revelation 22:17

<!-- LONG_ANSWER -->

Jesus gave the reason Himself: "Freely you have received" (Matthew 10:8). Nobody who preaches, teaches or counsels from Scripture paid for what he knows. The Word was handed to him, the Spirit opened his eyes to it, and other believers taught him. "We have not received the spirit of the world, but the Spirit who is from God, that we may understand what God has freely given us" (1 Corinthians 2:12).

A minister is therefore a steward, not an owner. "It is required of stewards that they be found faithful" (1 Corinthians 4:2). A steward who sells his master's goods for his own benefit is not faithful, even if the price is modest.

There is also the nature of the message. God's invitation has always been "come, buy wine and milk without money and without cost!" (Isaiah 55:1). The Bible ends the same way: "let the one who is thirsty come, and the one who desires the water of life drink freely" (Revelation 22:17). When the delivery of that invitation is put behind a price, the form of the ministry contradicts its content.
